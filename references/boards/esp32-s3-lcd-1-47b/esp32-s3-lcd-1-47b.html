ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B

An ESP32-S3 development board with a 1.47 inch IPS display, a 6-axis IMU and a microSD slot.

  • ESP32-S3R8
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • 1.47 in IPS, 172 x 320 px
  • Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth 5 LE
  • USB-C, 5 V

Overview

The Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B is a development board that uses the ESP32-S3R8 microcontroller with 8 MB PSRAM and 16 MB flash. The board carries a 1.47 inch IPS TFT display with 172 x 320 pixels, a QMI8658 6-axis IMU and a microSD slot. Its USB-C port supplies 5 V power, provides the native USB Serial/JTAG console and loads firmware.

What is on the board

  • MCU

    ESP32-S3R8

    QFN56 package with two Xtensa LX7 cores at up to 240 MHz. 512 KB SRAM and 384 KB ROM. 8 MB PSRAM in octal mode at 80 MHz in the vendor ESP-IDF demo. 16 MB flash, part W25Q128JVSI. Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n and Bluetooth 5 LE. The chip is bare; the board has no shield can.

    Internal flash and PSRAM. Onboard ceramic antenna.

  • Display

    ST7789

    1.47 inch IPS TFT, 172 x 320 pixels, 262K colours. 4-wire SPI in mode 0 with a separate DC pin. The vendor demo calls the driver ST7789T. The panel has no touch controller.

    SPI. Backlight on GPIO46 through a Q4 SI2302 low-side switch.

  • IMU

    QMI8658

    6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope. I2C at 400 kHz with 10K pull-ups. The vendor demo uses address 0x6B; the driver header also defines 0x6A.

    I2C. SCL GPIO47, SDA GPIO48.

  • microSD

    TF socket

    All four data lines are wired and fitted with 10K pull-ups. Both vendor demos use 1-bit mode; all four data lines are wired. The socket has no card detect signal.

    SDMMC. CLK 14, CMD 15, D0 16, D1 18, D2 17, D3 21.

  • RGB LED

    WS2812B-0807

    One addressable LED on the 3.3 V rail. The ESP-IDF demo drives it with RMT at 10 MHz. The Arduino demo uses neopixelWrite.

    Addressable RGB. DIN GPIO38.

  • Battery charger

    ETA6098

    Switching charger with a 2.2 uH inductor on the SW pin. ISET uses R5 82K. The CHG LED shows the charge state.

    3.7 V lithium cell. Charge from USB-C 5 V.

  • USB-C

    Native USB Serial/JTAG

    USB 2.0 full speed with VID:PID 303a:1001. VBUS is 5 V. CC1 and CC2 each have a 5.1K pull-down.

    Power input, console and firmware upload on one connector.

  • Buttons

    BOOT and RESET

    BOOT is GPIO0, active low, with a 10K pull-up. RESET drives CHIP_PU (EN), active low, with a 10K pull-up and a 1 uF capacitor. RESET is a hardware reset, not a GPIO.

    BOOT enters download mode. RESET restarts the chip.

Pinout

All GPIO signals use the 3.3 V rail. The sources do not state 5 V tolerance. The header has 18 pins at 2.54 mm pitch. The LCD, IMU, microSD, RGB LED and battery sense pins are not on the header.

USB Serial/JTAG

The USB-C port connects to the native USB Serial/JTAG controller. The board has no USB-UART bridge. The console is the USB Serial/JTAG port at 115200 baud, 8N1. VID:PID is 303a:1001.

An ESP-IDF project enables CONFIG_ESP_CONSOLE_USB_SERIAL_JTAG=y to put the console on this port. The board's serial port is the USB Serial/JTAG interface.

Console

UART0 TX is GPIO43 and UART0 RX is GPIO44 on the header. The sources do not state the UART0 baud rate. The USB Serial/JTAG interface carries the console.

Power

USB-C supplies 5 V on VBUS. VBUS feeds the charger U1 ETA6098 and the diode D1 MBR230LSFT1G. D1 connects VBUS to VSYS. The P-channel MOSFET Q1 AO3401 connects the battery to VSYS. The LDO U5 ME6217C33M5G converts VSYS to 3.3 V.

Battery voltage

The ADC reads VBAT through R1 200K on the top and R4 100K on the bottom. The divider ratio is 3.0. The ADC is ADC1 channel 0 on GPIO1, with 12 dB attenuation and 12-bit conversion. The calibration factor is 0.992857.

BAT_analogVolts = ADC_mV * 3.0 / 1000.0 / 0.992857

Charger

The ETA6098 switching charger drives the SW pin into a 2.2 uH inductor L1. BATS connects to BAT. ISET uses R5 82K. STAT drives LED1 through R3 3K. The charge current is not stated.

LDO

U5 ME6217C33M5G has VIN on VSYS, VOUT on 3V3 and EN tied to VIN. C4 0.1 uF and C5 10 uF are on the input. C3 10 uF is on the output. The output is rated 800 mA maximum.

5V header pin. The pin is VBUS through R30 0R. R31, the 0R path to VSYS, is not fitted. The pin therefore has no power when the board runs from the battery.

Quick start

The board is programmed over its USB-C serial port and runs from USB power or a 3.7 V battery.

SettingValue
Serial portUSB Serial/JTAG, 115200 baud, 8N1
Download modeHold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT

Upload

pio run -t upload --upload-port <serial-port>

Enter download mode first: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT. The USB Serial/JTAG interface ignores DTR and RTS. PlatformIO upload flags are --before=no_reset and --after=watchdog_reset.

Monitor

pio device monitor --port <serial-port> --baud 115200

Firmware and tooling

Vendor Arduino demos

  • Arduino IDE with the esp32 by Espressif Systems board package version 3.0.2 or later.
  • Board selection: ESP32S3 Dev Module. Enable USB CDC on boot for a USB-only board.
  • Libraries: LVGL 8.3.10, PNGdec 1.0.2 and OneButton.
  • LVGL_Arduino tests the display, IMU, RGB LED, microSD, battery and wireless functions.
  • LCD_Image shows PNG files from the microSD card root directory at intervals.

Vendor ESP-IDF demo

  • Project ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B-Test for ESP-IDF 4.4 or later.
  • LVGL 8.3.11 and espressif/led_strip 2.4.1.
  • Custom LCD driver Vernon_ST7789T.
  • sdkconfig.defaults enables octal PSRAM at 80 MHz, 16 MB flash, LVGL widgets, Bluetooth and BLE 5.0 features.
  • The demo initializes the display, IMU, RGB LED, microSD, battery, buttons and Wi-Fi.

PlatformIO

  • Board id esp32-s3-devkitc-1. The vendor archive has no PlatformIO board definition.
  • Platform espressif32@7.1.3, framework espidf, monitor speed 115200.
  • Upload flags --before=no_reset and --after=watchdog_reset.
